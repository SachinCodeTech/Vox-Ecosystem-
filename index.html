About Vox Ecosystem

Version 1.2.0 · CodeTech Developers

Vox Ecosystem is a unified launcher for the Vox family of applications. Instead of remembering separate links, you get one installable home screen that opens each tool in a tap.

What's inside

  • VoxCADD — browser-based CAD drafting and design workspace.
  • Arch Pad Pro — a digital sketch pad for architectural ideas.
  • 10to7 — productivity and work management.
  • Interior Bot — AI interior design, coming soon.

Independent by design

Each application is built and hosted independently. Vox Ecosystem simply connects them, so one app never depends on another to work.

Built by

CodeTech Developers · Lead Developer: Sachin Sheth.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Vox Ecosystem, provided by CodeTech Developers, is a launcher that links to separate applications. This policy covers the launcher itself.

The launcher does not require an account and does not collect, store or transmit personal information. It stores only the files needed to work offline on your device.

The apps you open from here (VoxCADD, Arch Pad Pro, 10to7 and others) are independent and open in your browser. Each has its own data handling, and any data you enter is subject to that app's own policy.
